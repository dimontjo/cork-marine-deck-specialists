## Homepage wireframe and draft copy

### 1. Hero
**A Better Decking Experience. Naturally.**

Transform your vessel with Jelinek AquaCork marine decking—a natural cork option designed for traction, comfort, and distinctive yacht styling.

CTA: **Request a Decking Consultation**
Secondary CTA: **Explore AquaCork Benefits**

Use licensed, genuine AquaCork installation photography where possible.

### 2. Why AquaCork?
**Comfort that belongs on the water.**
- **Cooler under the sun:** Highlight manufacturer-described thermal comfort; avoid numerical temperature comparisons without testing.
- **Traction when wet:** Describe the manufacturer's non-skid positioning; do not promise slip-proof performance.
- **Comfort underfoot:** Cork's resilient feel and noise/vibration dampening.
- **Natural appearance:** Warm, premium finish.
- **Renewable material:** Cork bark can be harvested without cutting down the cork oak tree.

### 3. Material comparison
Present AquaCork alongside EVA foam, PVC synthetic teak, and traditional teak. Compare appearance, comfort, maintenance, installation, and verified manufacturer claims. Avoid unsupported claims that AquaCork always outlasts alternatives.

### 4. Professional installation
**Precision from planning to the final seam.**
1. Consultation and vessel assessment
2. Deck measurement and layout planning
3. Substrate inspection and preparation
4. Installation using manufacturer-approved materials and procedures
5. Final walkthrough and care guidance

### 5. Applications
Motor yachts, cruising yachts, sportfishing boats, sailboats, cockpit and aft decks, swim platforms, walkways, and steps. Confirm substrate and application suitability with Jelinek.

### 6. Gallery
Before-and-after projects, deck details, edge finishing, seams, and installation process photos. Use actual work only; clearly label conceptual imagery.

### 7. Warranty and product assurance
**Quality Materials. Clear Warranty Information.**

We provide the applicable Jelinek AquaCork manufacturer warranty terms and care instructions when confirmed.

**Publication hold:** Do not state a specific AquaCork warranty duration, guaranteed 15–20 year life, or labor coverage until Jelinek confirms in writing. General Jelinek terms and separate indoor cork flooring warranties are not proof of AquaCork marine coverage.

Ask Jelinek for: written AquaCork-specific warranty; duration and exclusions; UV/saltwater/wet-deck coverage; approved adhesives and installation conditions; whether removal/reinstallation labor is covered; supported service life; photo/logo usage permission; dealer/installer program.

### 8. Final CTA
**Ready to Transform Your Deck?**

Tell us about your vessel and the area you'd like to upgrade.

CTA: **Request a Decking Consultation**

## Estimate form fields
Name, email, phone, vessel make/model/year, length, marina/city, decking area, current material, approximate square footage (optional), photos, desired timing, and notes. Add privacy consent and accessible validation.

## Design direction
Premium mobile-first aesthetic: deep navy, natural cork tan, warm ivory, restrained teal accents, large authentic yacht photos, clear typography, and prominent calls to action.

## Initial SEO plan
Target relevant phrases naturally: cork boat decking Florida, AquaCork installer, yacht decking replacement, marine cork decking, natural teak alternative. Create local pages only for areas actually served, starting with Stuart, Jupiter, West Palm Beach, and Fort Lauderdale if coverage is confirmed.

## Before launch
- Confirm AquaCork warranty and marketing permissions directly with Jelinek.
- Verify installation methods, substrate requirements, colors, thicknesses, maintenance instructions, and availability.
- Confirm business service territory, contact details, credentials, and insurance.
- Obtain rights to all photography and trademarks.
- Replace all draft claims with manufacturer-supported copy.
