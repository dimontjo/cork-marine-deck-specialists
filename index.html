Why AquaCork

Comfort that belongs on the water.

A thoughtfully selected marine decking material can change the way your boat looks and feels.

01 / COMFORT

Comfort underfoot

Cork's resilient character offers a welcoming surface for time spent aboard.

02 / TRACTION

Designed for wet decks

Jelinek describes AquaCork as a non-skid marine decking option. No surface is slip-proof.

03 / CLIMATE

Thermal comfort

Explore the manufacturer-described thermal benefits of cork in sunny marine environments.

Natural warmth

Warm cork tones bring a distinctive, refined appearance to your vessel.

05 / QUIET

Sound and vibration

Cork is valued for its sound- and vibration-dampening properties.
